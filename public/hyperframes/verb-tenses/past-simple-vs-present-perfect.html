Verb Tenses · Visual Lesson

Past time or present connection?

Common mistake

I have seen it yesterday.

The word “yesterday” closes the time in the past. That asks for Past Simple, not Present Perfect.

Past Simple

finished past time

I saw it yesterday.

The event is complete and separated from now.

Present Perfect

past connected to now

I have seen it before.

The experience matters now; the exact finished time is not the point.

Use Past Simple when the time is finished: yesterday, last week, in 2024.

Use Present Perfect when the past creates a result, experience, or connection now.
